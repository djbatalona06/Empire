DealerDoor is an app run by [DEALER LEGAL NAME], an independent authorized retailer of AT&T services in SeaTac, Washington ("we", "us"). We are not AT&T. This page explains what information the DealerDoor apps and website collect, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Contact details: name, email address, phone number and home address, when you sign up for service or when a sales representative enters them for you.
  • Consent records: the time, the exact wording you agreed to, who asked, and the address, so we can prove you agreed to be contacted.
  • Signatures and photos: a signature on a sign-up, a photo of the door where the visit happened, and an optional photo you attach to feedback.
  • Location (sales representatives only): the phone's location at the moment of a door check-in, to confirm the representative is at the address. We do not track location in the background.
  • Orders and payments: your order status and the dealer invoices we send you. Card and bank details are entered into Stripe's secure form. They never reach our servers.
  • Feedback and notifications: ratings and comments you write, and a device token so we can send you install and payment updates.

Why we use it

  • To set up your order, schedule the installation and bill the dealer fees.
  • To follow the rules for door-to-door sales and telemarketing consent.
  • To keep the do-not-knock list, so we do not visit homes that asked us not to.
  • To improve our service from your feedback.

Who sees it

Our staff who need it for their job, and the companies that run our service: Cloudflare (hosting and storage in the United States), Stripe (payments), and our email and notification providers. We do not sell your information. AT&T receives the details needed to place and activate your service; AT&T's own privacy policy covers what it does with them.
